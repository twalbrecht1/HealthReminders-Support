Common Questions

Using Health Reminders

How do I schedule an appointment?

Open a reminder, tap Schedule, turn on Scheduled, choose the date and time, and tap Save.

How do I add an appointment to Calendar?

Edit the reminder and use Add to Calendar beneath the appointment date. Allow Calendar access when prompted.

Why am I not receiving alerts?

Open Health Reminders and tap the bell button. Also confirm notifications are enabled for Health Reminders in the Settings app.

How does device syncing work?

Use the same Apple Account with iCloud enabled on each device. Updates can take a short time to appear after a device reconnects.

How do I stop scheduling suggestions?

Open Settings from the gear button and turn off Scheduling Popups. You can also select No, Don't Ask Again for one reminder.

How do I change the reminder order?

Open Settings and choose Alphabetic, Due Date, or Scheduled Date under Sort Order.
